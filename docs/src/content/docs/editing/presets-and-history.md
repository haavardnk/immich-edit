---
title: Presets, history, and versions
description: Save presets, copy and paste selective edits, compare snapshots, and manage versions.
---

Every edit step is recorded automatically so you can experiment freely, compare directions, and
reuse looks across shoots.

## Presets

Open **Presets** to browse saved looks as a thumbnail strip. Hover a preset (desktop) to preview it
on the current photo before applying.

- **Save preset**: store the current photo's edits under a name, choosing which sections to include.
- **Amount**: scale an applied preset from 0% to 200% to dial a look back or push it further.
- **Import / Export**: back up or share presets as JSON files.
- **Batch apply**: apply any preset to a multi-photo selection from the grid.

## Copy and paste edits

Transfer edits between photos without creating a preset:

- **Copy settings** copies the current photo's edits; opt in to **Masks** or **Retouch** when you
  want local adjustments included.
- **Paste** applies everything copied; **Paste only…** lets you pick individual sections (such as
  White Balance, Tone, or Lens Blur — note that Lens Blur copies settings, not the generated depth
  map).
- Works both from photo to photo in the editor and onto a bulk selection in the grid.

## History, snapshots, and versions

Find **History**, **Snapshots**, and **Versions and copies** in the inspector or under **More
editor actions** on touch:

- **History**: click any previous step to jump back; undo (`Ctrl+Z`) and redo (`Ctrl+Shift+Z`) step
  through changes.
- **Snapshots**: press `Ctrl+Shift+S` to save a named checkpoint of your current edits.
- **Versions and copies**: switch between the master photo and any virtual copies, or create a new
  copy from the current state.

## Before and after compare

- Hold `\` (or **Hold for original**) for an instant look at the unedited RAW.
- Press `Y` to cycle **Split view**, **Side by side**, and off.
- Choose what appears on the "before" side: the original file, any saved **Snapshot**, or another
  **Version**.
