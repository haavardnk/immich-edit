---
title: First edit
description: Open a photo, rate it, develop a RAW file, and export it back to Immich.
---

Every edit saves automatically to immich-edit's local database. Your original in Immich is never
modified.

## 1. Pick a photo

Open **Photos** or an album from the sidebar, then click or tap a photo to open the loupe. Press
`1`–`5` to rate, `P` to favorite, or `X` to mark a reject. Press `D` or tap **Edit** to enter the
editor.

## 2. Develop the RAW

In **Develop**, press `Ctrl+U` or click **Auto** for a balanced starting point on white balance and
tone. Refine **Exposure**, **Highlights**, **Shadows**, and **Color** to taste.

Hold `\` (or **Hold for original**) to compare against the unedited RAW, or press `Y` for split
and side-by-side views.

## 3. Straighten or mask

Switch to **Crop** (`Shift+R`) to frame or level the horizon, or open **Masks** to brighten a
subject or darken a sky without touching the rest of the frame.

## 4. Export

Open **Export** (or **Share** on phone and tablet). Choose a format and destination:

- **Upload to Immich** adds the finished photo to your library, optionally stacked with the
  original or placed in an album.
- **Download** saves the file directly to your device.

See the [editing workspace](../editing/workspace.md) to learn the layout and power tools.
